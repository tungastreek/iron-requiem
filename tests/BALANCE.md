# Campaign balance notes

The initial data run and final runs use different play policies. The initial bot advanced aggressively and used automatic counterattacks. The final bot spends spirits legally, screens vulnerable leaders, and uses prepared chapter levels, upgrades, and parts. Final runs use three seeds. The comparison shows coverage progress across the actual campaign rules and cannot isolate balance changes from bot changes.

The build staggers the Herald’s approach and makes its strongest gravity shot cost EN and require Will. Aurelion and the Hollow keep long range and high damage but must hold position to fire their largest beams. The original early-stage target was 5–7 turns, and the late-stage target was 8–12. The simulated wins are usually shorter, so player pacing targets still need a human playthrough.
