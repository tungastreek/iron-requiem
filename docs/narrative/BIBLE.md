# Narrative bible

## The premise

In Concord Year 0089, humanity has survived three wars by concentrating power and deleting inconvenient records. Kestrel Valley's Ashfall killed 41,000 people ten years earlier. The official explanation is a meteor. The survivor who became the Alteon pilot remembers light coming out of the ground. When the valley splits open a second time, independent cruiser Halcyon takes on that pilot, Mira Solenne and a duty it cannot carry alone.

Soren Kasuga built Alteon around a seed of the Aurora Heart, an older human technology that holds patterns of memory. **Resonants** hear and answer those patterns. Soren's voice in the awakened core is a surviving pattern of his mind. It can learn and make choices, but there are things it does not remember. We should never use it to undo his disappearance or give the protagonist a perfect parent.

The **Ourobor** were planted as guardians by the Aurorans before they left Earth. Their migrations, nursery defense and distress calls account for much of what humans mistake for aggression. They can kill people and can make bad choices. They do not become obedient on making contact. Peace requires space, safe channels and repairing harm.

The **Vael** are the Aurorans' descendants, remade in transit. Individual Vael can choose to couple their senses to the Choir. Fleeing the **Silence** turned that voluntary practice into a survival doctrine. The Silence copies signals and unwrites isolated resonant patterns. It borrows the voices people most want to hear. It cannot improvise, consent or revise a message after someone answers differently.

## What the story asks

Survival can cost the very thing people were trying to save. Ilse saved a chain of command by obeying at Lethe. Kroll is willing to preserve a population sample. Aurelion has kept the Ark intact by making the Choir too uniform to resist the dark. Each has good evidence their fear is justified. The question is which costs they acknowledge and whose permission they ask.

A chorus is made of voices retaining their differences. Don't put this thesis in every character's mouth. Show a witness declining to be named, an enemy changing a course because refugees refuse to board, a worker deciding who gets the tools. Victory pays off those acts. The route split asks whose immediate emergency receives the Halcyon's finite time. Neither route sees everything.

## Rhythm and voice

- Earnest, hopeful, practical. Grief interrupts people mid-task and returns later. It never becomes a speech requirement before a fight.
- Operational calls are usually one sentence. A conversation can have a long admission after trust is earned. Split exposition across disagreement, a physical clue and its consequence.
- Use one image a character would observe. Noor notices a packet timestamp. Corin hears pressure climbing. Emi notices a door that can't be opened by anyone inside.
- Humor comes from shared history or incompatible habits. Brann can be funny about a repair while taking a death seriously. Never send a joke to stand in for a reaction to a casualty.
- A choice states an action and its immediate stake. Avoid labels like Good, Evil or True Route. Let later speakers show what it cost. Keep alternatives plausible for a responsible commander.
- Cut-in lines interrupt action, arrive before the strongest impact, and fit one breath. A first meeting has a reply. Repeated basic attacks take generic tactical calls.
- Keep names and pronouns consistent with the selected protagonist. Script text uses {P} for the given name. Rina has the same past and duties as Rei. Neither frame choice grants an ending advantage.

## Continuity anchors

Keep Solenne's death at Kestrel, the evidence of Lethe in Ilse's own words, Okonkwo's relationship to Brann, Sera's choice to leave the Choir, the core's change to Alteon Zero and the Heart beneath the Antarctic ice. The Gate makes the system visible to the Silence whichever ending the player earns. The former imperial plan takes forty thousand. The new alliance shares decision-making, risk and infrastructure.

The two route crises happen in parallel. Reports about the other route can be incomplete. An Earth-route Yuna or Space-route Trinity may speak as a remote contact once introduced, but a remote contact never has an equipped machine, combat line, or roster epilogue. Use on-board alternates for previously unconditional late dialogue.
